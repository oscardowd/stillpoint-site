Privacy Policy

Stillpoint is built to keep your focus data close to you.

This policy describes the current behavior of the app as implemented in the public Stillpoint codebase.

What Stillpoint stores

Stillpoint stores block lists, schedules, streak state, local PIN hashes, onboarding choices, and other app state on-device using Apple's local storage APIs and the app's App Group container.

Stillpoint does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or custom server telemetry in the current implementation.

Apple services

Stillpoint can use Apple's Screen Time, notifications, StoreKit, optional Sign in with Apple, and iCloud account-status checks. Purchases and Apple identity flows are handled by Apple.

What Stillpoint does not do

  • No third-party ad tracking.
  • No sale of personal information.
  • No custom remote analytics pipeline in the current release branch.
  • No collection of contact lists, photos, location, or health data.

Future changes

If Stillpoint adds remote sync, analytics, crash reporting, or additional account systems in a future release, this policy and the App Store privacy answers should be updated before that build ships.
